
# Research Questions

### 1.Where are Kenya's major forests located, and how are they distributed geographically?

- Interactive forest map, county/region filters.

### 2.What wildlife species have been recorded in and around Kenya's forests?

- Species counts, searchable species list, occurrence map using GBIF data.

### 3.Which forests or regions have the highest recorded biodiversity?

- Ranking forests/regions by species richness and number of observations.

### 4.What is the conservation status of species found in Kenya's forests?

- Whether endangered, vulnerable, near threatened, least concern, etc., using conservations data where available.

### 5.How have wildlife observations changed over time across Kenya?

- Trends by year, species, region, or forest.

### 6.What environmental conditions characterize different forest areas?

- Comparing temperature, rainfall/weather, elevation, or other available environmental variables.



# Forest Explorer Kenya Logical Journey

**Where are the forests?→ What lives there?→ Where is biodiversity highest?→ What is threatened?→ How is it changing?→ What conditions shape these ecosystems?**
