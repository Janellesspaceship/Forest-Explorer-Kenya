### 1. What is the problem you are solving?

Environmental information about Kenya's forests and wildlife is scattered across multiple government websites, conservation organizations, and biodiversity databases. This makes it difficult for tourists, students, researchers, conservationists, and policymakers to access accurate, up-to-date information from a single source.

Forest Explorer Kenya solves this by building a centralized platform that automatically collects, processes, and presents environmental data in an accessible and interactive way.



### 2. What tools did you use?

Forest Explorer Kenya leverages modern data engineering and full-stack technologies to build an automated end-to-end data platform.

**Data Engineering**

**Python** – Data extraction, transformation, and automation

**Pandas** – Data cleaning and preprocessing

**Apache Airflow** – Workflow orchestration and ETL scheduling

**Google Cloud Storage** – Raw data storage

**Google BigQuery** – Cloud data warehouse

**PostgreSQL** – Application database

**Backend**

**FastAPI** – REST API development

**Frontend**

**React.js** – Responsive user interface

**Leaflet.js** – Interactive forest and wildlife maps

**APIs & Data Sources**

**GBIF API** – Wildlife and biodiversity data

**IUCN Red List API**– Conservation status

**OpenWeather API** – Weather information

**Kenya Forest Service datasets** – Forest information

**Development & Deployment**
- GitHub
- Docker
- Google Cloud Platform


### 3. What insights did you or do you want to discover?/ What Solutions do you want to offer? Do People even need these solutions?

Forest Explorer Kenya transforms scattered environmental data into meaningful insights that support conservation, education, and eco-tourism.

The platform enables users to:

- Explore forests and protected areas across Kenya.
- Discover wildlife species and their conservation status.
- Visualize biodiversity trends through interactive dashboards.
- Compare environmental data across regions.
- Access current weather conditions for different forests.
- Search and filter forests and wildlife from a single platform.

Rather than simply displaying data, the project provides actionable information that can improve awareness, research, and environmental decision-making.




### 4. How would a business or a community (for Social Impact Projects) benefit from your work?

Forest Explorer Kenya creates value for multiple stakeholders.

**Conservation Organizations**
- Improve biodiversity monitoring.
- Support conservation planning with centralized data.
- Identify trends that require intervention.

**Researchers & Students**
- Access reliable environmental data from one platform.
- Reduce time spent collecting data from multiple sources.
- Support research and academic projects.

**Tourists & Eco-tourism Operators**
- Discover forests and wildlife destinations.
- Plan visits using location and weather information.
- Promote sustainable tourism.

**Government & Policymakers**
- Support evidence-based environmental planning.
- Monitor conservation efforts.
- Improve public access to environmental information.

**Community Impact**
- Increase environmental awareness.
- Promote conservation education.
- Encourage responsible tourism and protection of Kenya's natural heritage.
